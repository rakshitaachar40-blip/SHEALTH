# SHEALTH 🩷

### Because Women's Health Should Never Be a Taboo.

**Breaking the silence. Protecting privacy. Empowering women to understand their health.**

SHEALTH is a women's healthcare and wellness platform designed to make health tracking, medical education, and consultation preparation more accessible, understandable, and privacy-conscious.

We believe that every woman deserves to understand her body without shame, access trustworthy health information without fear, and seek medical support without feeling unheard.

Our mission is simple: **turn silence into awareness, uncertainty into understanding, and healthcare into a more informed and empowering experience.**

---

## 🌸 The Problem: A Conversation That Often Never Happens

"Is this normal?"

"Should I be worried?"

"What if someone finds out?"

"Is it okay to talk about this?"

For many women, these questions are not just about health. They are about embarrassment, stigma, privacy, and the fear of being judged.

Menstruation, irregular periods, reproductive health, hormonal changes, and intimate health concerns can be difficult to discuss openly. In many families and communities, these subjects are still surrounded by silence or treated as inappropriate conversations.

A young woman experiencing unusual menstrual symptoms may hesitate to ask for help because she has never been encouraged to discuss them. Someone struggling to understand recurring symptoms may search the internet and encounter confusing, contradictory, or unreliable information. Another woman may know she needs medical advice but feel uncomfortable explaining sensitive details or sharing her health history.

These barriers can make it harder to recognise when professional guidance is needed, keep track of recurring symptoms, and prepare for a meaningful conversation with a healthcare professional.

### The challenges we want to address

* **The stigma surrounding women's health:** Menstruation and reproductive health should be normal healthcare conversations, not sources of shame.
* **The information gap:** Finding understandable, reliable, and relevant women's health information can be difficult.
* **The challenge of tracking symptoms:** Without a consistent record, it may be harder to recognise patterns or explain symptoms during a consultation.
* **Privacy and security concerns:** Health records can contain deeply personal information. Users should understand how their information is handled and have meaningful control over what they choose to share.
* **Barriers to seeking help:** Uncertainty, embarrassment, and difficulty preparing for consultations may discourage some people from asking questions or seeking appropriate care.

**Women's health is not a luxury, an embarrassment, or a conversation that should be whispered. It is healthcare—and it deserves attention, dignity, and respect.**

---

## 💗 Our Solution: Meet SHEALTH

SHEALTH brings women's health tracking, educational resources, health insights, reminders, and consultation preparation together in one digital platform.

Instead of leaving users to piece together their health history across notes, searches, and scattered information, SHEALTH aims to provide a more organised place to record symptoms, explore educational material, review potential patterns, and prepare questions for healthcare professionals.

We want users to feel more informed about their health—not pressured to self-diagnose, frightened by online searches, or forced to share personal information without understanding their choices.

### Our approach

**Track. Understand. Learn. Prepare. Connect.**

* **Track:** Record symptoms and review previous entries.
* **Understand:** Explore symptom history and potential patterns.
* **Learn:** Access educational content about women's health.
* **Prepare:** Organise information and questions before a medical consultation.
* **Connect:** Explore a proposed doctor-discovery and consent-based information-sharing experience.

SHEALTH is designed to support informed healthcare decisions, not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🩸 Symptom Tracking

Users can record symptoms and review their history over time.

A consistent record can help users organise their observations and communicate their experiences more clearly during a medical consultation.

### 2. 📊 Health Insights

SHEALTH provides a way to review symptom history and explore potential patterns in recorded information.

These insights are intended to support awareness and reflection. Patterns alone cannot establish a medical diagnosis.

### 3. 📚 Women's Health Education

The educational section presents articles intended to help users learn more about women's health topics.

Our goal is to make health information easier to understand and encourage users to seek qualified medical guidance when needed.

Medical content should be reviewed for accuracy, supported by credible sources, and kept up to date as the project develops.

### 4. 🤖 AI-Assisted Insights

The existing application includes an optional Anthropic API integration for AI-generated summaries.

When configured and functioning correctly, AI assistance can help present information in a more accessible format.

AI-generated content must not be treated as a confirmed diagnosis or a substitute for clinical evaluation. A future improvement is to ground relevant responses in a curated, medically reviewed knowledge base with clear source references.

### 5. 🔔 Health Reminders

SHEALTH includes reminder functionality to help users organise health-related routines and follow-ups.

### 6. 🩺 Doctor Support

The doctor-support section helps users prepare for consultations by organising questions and supporting health-report generation.

SHEALTH Connect explores how a women's health platform could make it easier to discover suitable healthcare professionals while giving users clearer choices about personal information sharing.

## 🔒 Privacy, Security, and Trust

Women's health information can be deeply personal. A platform that supports women must treat privacy as a core responsibility—not an optional feature.

SHEALTH's design direction prioritises transparency, informed choices, and responsible handling of health information.

### Our privacy principles

**1. Choice before sharing**

Users should understand what information they are sharing, with whom, and why. The SHEALTH Connect prototype demonstrates selectable report-sharing preferences.

**2. Purposeful information sharing**

A future implementation should share only the information authorised for a specific purpose, rather than automatically exposing an entire health history.

**3. Clear identity expectations**

Users should be able to understand when identity disclosure may be required. We do not promise anonymous medical consultations; professional, clinical, payment, and legal requirements may necessitate identification.

**4. Responsible use of AI**

AI-generated summaries should communicate uncertainty appropriately, avoid unsupported diagnostic claims, and make relevant limitations clear.

**5. Protection of sensitive information**

A production implementation requires appropriate authentication, access controls, secure transmission and storage, consent enforcement, and auditability.

**6. Transparency about limitations**

We distinguish between existing application functionality, prototype demonstrations, and future plans. We do not claim that a privacy control is secure merely because it appears in the interface.

### Current security limitations

SHEALTH is a hackathon prototype, not a production-ready medical platform.

The existing backend uses JSON-file-based persistence. The project has not been established as a fully audited or clinically validated healthcare service.

Before handling real patient information or connecting real healthcare professionals, the application would require a thorough security and privacy review, stronger access controls, appropriate data-retention practices, and compliance assessment for applicable requirements.

**We believe privacy is not just about hiding information. It is about giving people clarity, dignity, and meaningful control over their information.**

## 📚 Trusted Medical Knowledge: Evidence Before AI

Women's health deserves more than random internet searches or AI-generated answers without reliable evidence.

When someone has a question about menstruation, reproductive health, hormonal changes, or other sensitive health concerns, the information they receive should be understandable, responsible, and grounded in credible medical knowledge.

**SHEALTH's approach is to prioritise trustworthy health information from recognised public-health authorities and established healthcare resources.**

Our intended reference sources include:

* **[World Health Organization (WHO)](https://www.who.int/health-topics/sexual-health)** — global public-health guidance and information on sexual and reproductive health.
* **[National Health Service (NHS)](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/)** — patient-friendly information about health conditions, symptoms, and treatments.
* **[Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)](https://www.icmr.gov.in/)** — Indian biomedical research and health-related resources.
* **[MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/)** — health information from the US National Library of Medicine.

These sources provide a foundation for developing an educational knowledge base. Individual articles and recommendations should be selected for relevance, checked against their original sources, and reviewed for accuracy before being presented as medically grounded content.

### How SHEALTH aims to use medical information

1. **Source:** Identify relevant information from credible medical and public-health resources.
2. **Review:** Check the content, context, publication details, and relevance of the source.
3. **Organise:** Structure the information by women's health topics so it can be retrieved when needed.
4. **Explain:** Present health education in clear, accessible language.
5. **Reference:** Where the implementation supports it, provide links to the original sources so users can explore the evidence themselves.

Our goal is to make reliable health information easier to understand without removing the context or limitations that matter.

### Why this matters

AI can produce fluent answers that are incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate for an individual's circumstances. Using credible medical references can help reduce reliance on unsupported claims, but source selection alone does not guarantee that every answer is correct.

SHEALTH therefore aims to distinguish general health education from personalised medical advice and encourage consultation with qualified healthcare professionals when appropriate.

## 🤖 AI-Assisted Health Insights: AI as an Assistant, Not an Authority

SHEALTH includes an optional Anthropic API integration intended to support AI-generated summaries.

Our guiding principle is simple:

**AI should help make health information easier to understand—not replace medical evidence or professional judgement.**

The project's intended direction is to combine AI assistance with curated medical information from sources such as WHO, NHS, and other reputable health authorities.

A future source-grounded workflow could work as follows:

1. A user asks a health-related educational question.
2. The system retrieves relevant information from an approved medical knowledge base.
3. The retrieved information is supplied to the AI model as context.
4. The model generates an accessible explanation based on that material.
5. The application displays relevant source references when available.
6. The response communicates uncertainty and does not present itself as a diagnosis.

This approach is commonly known as **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**.

Unlike an AI response generated without retrieved references, a properly implemented RAG workflow can connect answers to selected source material. However, it still requires careful evaluation because retrieved information may be incomplete, outdated, or misinterpreted by the model.

### Current implementation and future development

The existing project includes an optional Anthropic API integration. The complete extent of medical-source retrieval, source citation, and grounding must be verified against the submitted code.

The source-grounded workflow described above is a development direction unless those capabilities are demonstrably implemented and tested.

SHEALTH does not claim that its AI is a medical professional, that its responses are always correct, or that its outputs can independently diagnose health conditions.

Our objective is to build a more transparent and responsible approach to AI-assisted women's health education, where credible sources inform the experience and users remain encouraged to seek appropriate professional care.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

### Prerequisites

* Node.js and npm.
* A copy of the SHEALTH repository.
* Any required environment configuration for optional integrations.

### 1. Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/rakshitaachar40-blip/SHEALTH.git
cd SHEALTH
```

### 2. Install backend dependencies

```bash
cd server
npm install
```

Start the backend using the script defined in the server's `package.json`. In the current project setup, the documented command is:

```bash
npm start
```

### 3. Install frontend dependencies

Open a separate terminal:

```bash
cd client
npm install
```

Start the Vite development server using:

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open the local address displayed by Vite in your browser.

### Configuration notes

Optional AI functionality may require an Anthropic API key configured through the appropriate server-side environment variable.

Never commit `.env` files, API keys, passwords, tokens, or other secrets to GitHub.

The installation instructions should be checked against the final submitted version of the project before judging.
